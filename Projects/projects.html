Before the Tee

Before the Tee is a top-down golf course management simulator game. You run the links, not the round. Design the course, set tee times, manage the cart fleet, drive the maintenance cart around to fix issues, and watch groups arrive to play their way through a full day from sunrise to sunset. 1500+ wishlists on steam planning for a Winter 2026 release.

ShowMe Cup

A group of friends decided to play local Ryder Cup style golf tournament. After 2 years of struggling to find a scoring app that can handle the format, I developed a website to fill the gap. Match creation, score card entry, leaderboards and stats easily accessible for the competitors in the tournament.
